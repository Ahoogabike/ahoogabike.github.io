Sales reporting and saved filters

How to get a number you can trust out of Odoo — and the two data defects that will otherwise mislead you.

1. Sales analysis

Sales → Orders → Quotations / Orders
1
The default view is My Quotations. Remove the filter to see everything.
2
Add the filter Sales Team = Point of Sale Louise to see your shop.
3
Add Group by → Salesperson to see who sold what. Add a Create date filter (e.g. after 1 January) to bound the period.
Quotations and sales orders are different thingsA list that includes quotations overstates your business. Filter on Sales Orders when you want real numbers.
Sales orders include repairsA repair is something you sell and deliver, so it appears as a sales order. Separate repairs from bike sales when you analyse.

2. Views to use

PivotSales orders per month; add Product Category as a grouping to see the mix.
GraphThe same data as a chart.
CalendarScheduled activities and expected delivery dates — filter by team first.
ListAdd columns: delivery date, expected date, taxes, tags, warehouse, invoice status.

3. Two known data defects

Check before you believe a figure 1. Wrong Sales Team. Some orders carry the wrong team — dealer orders attributed to the shop team and the other way round.
2. No individual salesperson. A large block of orders has "Ahooga flagship store" as the salesperson, i.e. nobody individual assigned.

When a figure looks odd, check both fields before believing it.
PreventionSet Salesperson = you and Sales Team = Point of Sale Louise on every order you create. Audit the month's orders once a month.

4. Building and sharing a filter

Example — all leasing orders:

4
Filters → Add custom filter → Customer is in → select your leasing partners.
5
Add Creation date is after 01/01/… so it stays valid next year.
6
Apply.
7
Favourites → Save current search → name it → tick Shared → Save.

From then on it is one click. Several shared filters already exist (for example Sales order B2C Belgium) — look at them to see how they were built.

5. Product-level analysis

8
Open a product → the header shows On Hand and units sold in the last 365 days (company-wide).
9
To get your figure, click the sold quantity and add Sales Team = Point of Sale Louise.
10
Click On Hand to see quantity per location and how much is reserved. Group by location to compare Louise Stock and the warehouse.

6. POS reporting

Point of Sale → Sessions / Orders / Reporting

Sessions give you per-day pickings, orders and payment totals; the picking colours give you your error list (see POS vs back office).
