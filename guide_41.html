Shop routines: daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly

The rhythm that keeps everything else in these guides true. Print it and put it next to the till.

Every morning

1
Open the POS register: count the cash, enter the opening balance. → Open and close the till
2
Open Project → Repair Louise and look at today: who is dropping a bike off, which bikes are due to be collected. → Take in a repair
3
Check the bikes to be handed over today: is the serial number assigned and the bike physically labelled with the customer and order number? → Confirm the order and prepare the bike
4
Check your Odoo notifications and activities, and Helpdesk → My Tickets. → Helpdesk tickets

Every evening

5
Move finished repairs to Waiting for customer to pick up — this sends the "ready" e-mail. → Move a repair through the board
6
Make sure every collection today went through the POS and every shipment through the back office — and that nothing went through both. → POS vs back office
7
Close the register: count cash against the expected figure.

Every week

8
Review red pickings across this week's POS sessions. → POS vs back office
9
Chase quotations: Quotation viewed but no answer → call. Use scheduled activities. → Create a quotation
10
Check the Typeform leasing leads and contact them. → Work the leasing leads
11
Review bikes in Waiting for customer to pick up older than 7 days and apply the pick-up policy. → Move a repair through the board
12
Review Missing component and Task blocked repairs — is anything actually being ordered?

Every month

13
Audit Waiting / Ready transfers from Louise Stock and clear the stale ones. → Stock discipline and stale transfers
14
Audit Sales Team and Salesperson on the month's orders, including orders with no individual salesperson. → Sales reporting
15
Review the accessory range and stock levels — are you carrying only what you can keep in stock? → Check stock before you promise anything
16
Review test-ride conversion using the Test ride / Walk-in test ride tags. → Create a quotation

Every quarter

17
Physical inventory of Louise Stock, including parts fitted to bikes awaiting collection. → Do a physical inventory count
18
Archive obsolete products — a smaller catalogue is a more accurate catalogue.
