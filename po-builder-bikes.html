Wheel assembly at MTB 3 weeks ahead of bikes
Each MAX bike needs one front + one rear wheel, assembled at MTB Maastricht (≈2 wk build + 1 wk ship). The wheels for a bike built in a given week must be assembled 3 weeks earlier. Wheels are ordered in batches of 150 front + 150 rear (300) per PO. Demand is driven by the bike plan in the “Plan to year-end” tab — adjust that and this updates.
