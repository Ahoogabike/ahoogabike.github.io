Work the leasing leads

The website generates leasing enquiries every month. They are the shop's leads — and they are currently not being worked.

1. Where the leads are

On the website, Services → Leasing offers two questionnaires: "I am an employee" and "I am an employer". The answers land in Typeform, accessible via the Ahooga Google account (ask Marketing / office for access).

Volume seen in trainingFor 2026 (partial year): roughly 15 employee requests and about 15 employer requests, one of them asking for 1 to 10 bikes. It was not clear that any of them had ever been contacted.

2. The routine

RuleThese are the shop's leads — nobody else owns them. Check Typeform on a fixed schedule (for example every Monday), and log each lead as a contact + activity in Odoo so it cannot be lost.
1
Open Typeform and list the new responses since your last check.
2
For each one, create or find the contact in Odoo — with country, language and e-mail, so any later quotation is correct.
3
Add a tag so leasing leads are findable as a group.
4
Schedule an activity (call or e-mail) with a date. An unscheduled lead is a lost lead.
5
Log what happened in a log note after contact: interested, not now, wrong fit, quoted.
6
Employer leads asking for several bikes deserve a call the same week — that is a fleet conversation, not a form reply.

3. Turning a lead into an order

Once the employer or leasing company is engaged, the order itself follows Write a leasing / B2B order: leasing company as customer, rider as delivery address, rider's name in Customer reference.

To be definedOwnership of the leasing leads and the weekly routine have historically been shared. This needs to be made explicit — see Open points and decisions.
