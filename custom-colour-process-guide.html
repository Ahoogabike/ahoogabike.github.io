Custom Colour Programme — Ordering Process

End-to-end workflow for ordering a custom-painted (MAX Custom Colour) frame — across Ahooga, Deco Coat and Velovisie. Each step lists its target and how responsibility is shared. Frames are scanned on the Deco Coat line, handed over to Velovisie with an automatic DSV label, scanned again on arrival, and paired to their serial at assembly — so stage, transfer and serial all stay live in the apps, and the production planner is told the moment a frame is ready to build.

01

Who's involved

Brand / Owner

Ahooga

Takes the sales order, processes it, plans production, and handles final delivery to the customer. Once a frame is painted, runs the Custom Colour Handover app to validate the purchase receipt, create the Anderlecht→Velovisie transfer and book the DSV label. HQ in Anderlecht (AH), Belgium.

Painting partner

Deco Coat

Paints the frame in the custom colour, then ships the painted frame plus configuration stickers onward. Scans each frame's stage on the line (waiting to paint → in painting → waiting to ship → shipped) so Ahooga sees live status and an estimated ship date. Contact: Wietze Hoppe (NL).

Assembly & logistics

Velovisie

Assembles the bike from the painted frame and configuration sticker, scans the arriving painted frame to validate its transfer (telling the planner the order is ready to build), assembles the bike, applies and registers the MACU······· frame serial (scanning the SO + serial to pair them), labels the box, and ships the finished bike back to Anderlecht.

03

Key documents & how to use them

Two Excel files do most of the work in the Order Processing phase (the planner app can generate these pre-filled from the live Odoo sales orders). From painting onward, the apps take over — the Handover app moves the frame to Velovisie, and the VV receipt scan feeds the PO Builder.

P200A Deco Coat Order Book

Decocoat Order book.xlsm
  1. Fill in the Configuration tab with the new order's details.
  2. Copy new orders across into the Order book tab (the running register).
  3. Copy this sheet into a new file — this is the file you attach to the email to Deco Coat.
  4. Generate the labels in the Labels_Print tab; its data is pulled automatically from the Order book tab.

P200B / P200C Purchase Order & Order Form

Order form.xlsx

The Purchase Order (PO) and the Order Form (Excel) are sent to Deco Coat alongside the labels. Columns: Prod Week, PO#, SO#, Bike SKU, Configuration, Quantity, Status, frame colours & finishes, logo colour/size, personal text, colour numbers and primers.

P400 Custom Colour Handover

cc-handover.html
  1. Open the app (Odoo login) — it lists the frames Deco Coat has marked waiting to ship.
  2. Tick one frame, or select all to send a pallet together, then Handover selected →.
  3. It validates the Deco Coat PO receipt, creates the Anderlecht → Velovisie transfer, books the DSV label and emails it to wietzehoppe@decocoat.nl (cc you).
  4. Grab the label yourself from the card or the History tab if needed — every handover is logged.

P450 Velovisie receipt & the production planner

vv-scan.html → 📦 Velovisie receipt · po-builder-bikes.html
  1. On arrival, open VV app → Velovisie receipt and scan the painted frame (or type the SO / serial).
  2. The app finds the matching transfer, validates it in Odoo, and posts the order to the shared registry as ready to build.
  3. In the PO Builder the order then appears in the 🎨 ready to build now lane on This week — schedule it, then hit Mark built.

05

Status of past open points

Where the programme's known gaps now stand.

✓ Serial ↔ Sales Order is now linked

At assembly, Velovisie scans the SO and the MACU······· serial (VV Serial Scan, step P550). The pairing is saved to a shared registry that the Custom Colors app reads, so any physical bike traces back to its SO — no manual sheet entry.

Owner & trigger: Velovisie, at assembly, for every frame — backup manual entry available in the same app and on the dashboard.

✓ Live line status & ship-date estimate

Deco Coat scans each frame's stage on the line (step P350). The Custom Colors app shows the live stage and auto-learns average stage durations to estimate when each frame will ship.

Note: estimates sharpen as more frames flow through and build up timing history.

✓ Handover & DSV label automated

When a frame is ready to ship, the Custom Colour Handover app validates the PO receipt, creates the Anderlecht → Velovisie transfer and books the DSV label — emailed straight to Deco Coat (and several frames can ride on one pallet). No manual transfer or label step.

Owner & trigger: Ahooga · Supply, per frame or per pallet (step P400).

✓ Arrival tells the planner what to build

Scanning the painted frame on arrival at Velovisie validates the transfer and surfaces that exact order in the PO Builder's “ready to build now” lane — production is scheduled from physically-present frames, not guesswork.

Owner & trigger: Velovisie scan on arrival (P450); planner schedules & marks built.

⚠ Back-fill historic serials

Frames produced before the scan system went live may still be missing their SO ↔ serial link.

Action: back-fill the older serials against their orders so the registry is complete from here on.

↻ Is the separate Order Form still needed?

Worth challenging whether the standalone Order Form (P200C) is still necessary, or whether the Order Book + PO already cover it — to discuss with Deco Coat.
