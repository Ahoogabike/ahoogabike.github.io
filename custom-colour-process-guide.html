Custom Colour Programme · Ordering Process

End-to-end workflow for a custom-painted MAX, from the customer's design to delivery, across Ahooga, Deco Coat and Velovisie. Each step lists its target, who is responsible and what Odoo or the apps do automatically. Day-to-day owner of the custom colour checks and alert emails: Thao.

01

Who's involved

Brand / Owner

Ahooga

Takes the sales order, checks the colour configuration, orders the paint from Deco Coat, books the frame transport to Velovisie and ships the finished bike to the customer. Custom colour checks and alerts: Thao.

Painting partner

Deco Coat

Paints the frame in the custom colour and scans each frame's stage on the line (waiting to paint → in painting → waiting to ship → shipped), so Ahooga sees live status and an estimated ship date. Painted frames + configuration stickers go straight to Velovisie on a DSV label booked by Ahooga. Contact: Wietze Hoppe (NL).

Assembly & shipping

Velovisie

Receives the painted frame, assembles the bike, applies and registers the MACU······· serial (SO + serial scan), labels the box and ships the finished bike directly to the customer, together with the order's accessories.

03

Emails, tools & special routes

What lands in your inbox, where to look, and the cases that don't follow the standard route.

Emails What arrives automatically (to Thao unless noted)

  1. Odoo activity "Custom colour: check manually before Deco Coat PO" · an order is not in the standard format (P150).
  2. "New custom order" · within ~30 min of each confirmed custom colour order.
  3. "Custom colour — morning check" · every day ~07:00 Brussels: only orders stuck at a gate (no PO after 24 h, 10 days at the painter, not confirmed on arrival…). One line if nothing is stuck.
  4. "Custom colour — N orders to approve" · weekdays ~14:00 Brussels: the daily batch for the Deco Coat PO (P200).
  5. "Painted frames ready to ship" · to supply@, Felix and Thao when Deco Coat scans a frame as waiting to ship (P400).

Tools Where to look

  1. Custom Colors app (internal tool) · "To order" batch, PO, order forms, labels, serial tracking.
  2. Custom Colour Handover · book the DSV label Deco Coat → Velovisie.
  3. Deco Coat board · every frame by stage on the paint line.
  4. Dealer portal → Rep overview → Supply → Custom colour orders · per order: client, Deco Coat PO, Velovisie PO, stage, next-stage date, ETA, serial, delivered, invoiced.
  5. Odoo tags on the SO · CC check = blocked, waiting for Thao · CC OK = checked and approved.

UK UK online orders (Shopify, team Online)

Not shipped from Velovisie to the customer. Route: Velovisie → Brain Log (receipt "VVIN", the bike is locked to its own SO by serial) → next big Brain Log → UK shipment → Derby 3PL receipt → UK delivery to the customer. UK dealer orders follow the standard route.

05

Status of open points

Where the programme's known gaps stand (25 Nov 2026).

✓ Wrong colours blocked before painting

The colour check (P150) stops any off-standard order from reaching Deco Coat until Thao has approved it with CC OK.

Note: deliberately strict · free notes such as "customer wants helmet…" also trigger a check.

✓ Serial ↔ Sales Order linked · live line status

Velovisie registers SO + serial at assembly; Deco Coat scans each stage. Both are visible in the Custom Colors app and the portal dashboard.

✓ Decal invoiced · accessories ship with the bike · lead time fixed

Personalised decal now follows the bike for invoicing; accessories are moved Brain Log → Velovisie automatically; custom colour lead time is fixed at 70 days (no longer overwritten by stock levels).

⚠ Velovisie delivery can pick the wrong custom bike

The Velovisie delivery takes whichever custom bike arrives first, not necessarily the one painted for that order. One likely swap was found (MACU0000157: S27741 vs S27133).

Action: before shipping, check the serial on the delivery matches the serial registered for that SO.

⚠ Accessories transfer from Brain Log

Once, Brain Log's warehouse system shipped accessories straight to the customer instead of to Velovisie (UK order S28134).

Action: if a customer gets accessories before the bike, tell Supply.

⚠ Small known issues

Colour names with brackets, e.g. "RAL 9002 (Grey White)", are misread as a finish in the Custom Colors app · older frames may still miss their SO ↔ serial link · 17 open orders still show the old 30–35 day lead time.
