Invoice from the back office

For shipped orders, B2B and leasing you invoice from the sales order rather than the till — and there is one line the system will not add for you.

When to invoice from the back office

Customer collects in the shopInvoice through the POS — see Settle an order and hand over at the till.
Goods are shippedInvoice from the back office (this guide) and validate the delivery in the back office.
B2B / dealerBack office, and send by Peppol.
Leasing companyBack office — never the POS. See Invoice a leasing company.

The procedure

1
On the sales order: Create Invoice → Regular invoice.
2
Add the labour / service line manually if the order contains any (see the warning below).
3
Check invoice address, invoice date, due date and payment terms.
4
Confirm the invoice.
5
Send it — e-mail for B2C, Peppol (and/or e-mail) for B2B and leasing.
6
Make sure the delivery transfer for the goods is separately validated, so the goods really leave your stock.
Warning — services do not appear on a back-office invoiceOdoo invoices from the back office only what can be delivered / shipped. Labour and maintenance are services, so they are not picked up automatically. You must add the labour line manually to the invoice before confirming it. This is a very easy line to forget, and forgetting it means giving the work away.
HabitBefore you confirm any back-office invoice, compare it line by line with the sales order. Missing lines are almost always services.
