Open and close the till

The first and last Odoo actions of every shop day, plus how to read the POS back office afterwards.

Every morning: open the register

Point of Sale → your register → Open register
1
Count the cash in the drawer.
2
Enter the amount as the opening balance.
3
Add a note if something needs explaining.
4
Open register. The till is now open and the shop can sell.

Every evening: close the register

Point of Sale → Close register
5
You see the cash opening amount, the cash movements of the day, the bank total, and the expected cash count.
6
Verify the physical cash against the expected figure.
7
Close the session.
Before you closeMake sure every collection today went through the POS and every shipment through the back office — and that nothing went through both. See POS vs back office.

The POS back office

Point of Sale → Orders / Sessions / Reporting
SessionsOne line per day, with the opening and closing balance and the status (Closed & Posted = all good). Open a session to see: number of pickings, number of orders, total payments.
OrdersEvery ticket, with its invoiced / posted / cancelled status. From an order you can reach its picking and its invoice.
ReportingSales analysis for the till.
Red pickings are your error listA red picking inside a session means the customer paid and took the goods, but the stock transfer was never validated. Review them weekly — the method is in POS vs back office: keep stock right and investigate a red picking.
To confirmReceipt printing: the shop has no receipt printer set up. This is a hardware/setup item for IT.
