Purchase orders to place — next 3 weeks only what the parts allow
Demand comes from the published MY27 Sales Forecast plus open sales orders whose delivery falls inside the production + transit window; each first-week PO is topped up to the minimum batch with free-stock filler. Every SKU is then checked against its own BoM — the colour- and rack-specific lines included — so a colour whose frame is not there is held back rather than proposed. Committed bikes take the last parts before a speculative stock build does.

Wheel assembly at MTB 3 weeks ahead of bikes
Each MAX bike needs one front + one rear wheel, assembled at MTB Maastricht (≈2 wk build + 1 wk ship). The wheels for a bike built in a given week must be assembled 3 weeks earlier. Wheels are ordered in batches of 150 front + 150 rear (300) per PO. Demand is driven by the bike plan in the “Plan to year-end” tab — adjust that and this updates.

Forward component stock — MY27 burn-down stock at start of production, depleted weekly
Each part starts at its projected stock at the start of MY27 production — i.e. the last column of the Parts Stock → Forward stock tab (what's left once the current MY26 plan has consumed its parts) — and is then depleted week by week as the MY27 plan builds bikes. Each cell is the stock remaining after that week's consumption (bikes/week × per-bike BoM usage, averaged per family). Click the Start figure to see how it's calculated; click a (+n) arrival to see which PO it comes from. A red / negative cell means the part runs out — that week is your order-by date. Per-bike usage is averaged across each family's bikes, so colour/rack-specific parts are approximate.
