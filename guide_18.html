Create a quotation

From the sales conversation to a clean quotation in Odoo — including the two product traps and the tab that decides whether your shop gets credited for the sale.

1. The sales conversation, before Odoo

RuleAt the end of a test ride, ask "Are you ready to purchase?" — not "Would you like a quotation?".

"Would you like a quotation?" gives the customer a binary choice between nothing and a piece of paper. "Are you ready to purchase?" lets them simply say yes, and many do. If they say no, you can still offer the quotation. When this change was introduced in the shop, sales rose by roughly 20–30%.

The same logic applies to accessories: ask. A customer leaving without a helmet is not only lost margin, it is a customer who has to come back for something they should have left with.

3. Adding products — the two big traps

Trap 1: creating a product by accident

When you type in a product line, Odoo offers matching products. If you type a product name and press Enter without selecting a real product from the list, Odoo creates a brand-new product with that name.

WarningA phantom product has no stock, no cost, no category, no routing and no accounting account — and it silently pollutes the catalogue and every stock and margin report. Always select an existing product from the dropdown. If you cannot find it, use Search more…, and if it truly does not exist, ask before creating anything.

Practical tips while searching:

  • Use Search more… to see the full list — the dropdown only shows the first few matches.
  • Drag the column border in the Search more dialog to widen the product-name column; variant names are long and otherwise unreadable.
  • Products have internal reference codes. Ahooga does not maintain EAN codes on products, so search by name or internal reference.

Trap 2: variants

Most Ahooga products exist as variants, and the variant is what carries the stock. For an E-MAX Alfine you must choose the right combination of, for example:

  • Colour: the standard colour, a custom colour, or a non-standard colour
  • With rack or without rack

Choosing "E-MAX Alfine" generically is not enough; pick the exact variant, then confirm the specific attribute values (colour, size) in the configurator pop-up.

Note on product structureIn 2026 the range is configured with standard / non-standard / custom colour variants. From next year the plan is one product line per colour, which will make selection simpler.

4. The "Other Info" tab — the most important tab in the quotation

Everything here is invisible on the printed quotation and yet it determines revenue reporting and stock movements. Fill it in every time.

FieldWhat to setWhy it matters
SalespersonYouIndividual sales reporting.
Sales TeamPoint of Sale LouiseIf this is wrong, the revenue is credited to another team and your shop's numbers are wrong. This error is already present in historical data.
Customer referencee.g. the rider's name on a leasing orderLets you find a leasing order by the person's name.
TagsTest ride (customer booked a test ride online) or Walk-in test ride (came in without an appointment)The two tags the shop should use. They let you measure conversion: how many booked test rides became sales, and how many walk-ins became sales. Tagging the bike model is no longer necessary.

Shipping policyWhen all products are ready or As soon as possibleAs soon as possible lets Odoo propose a partial delivery as soon as any line is available (deliver the bike now, the accessory later). When all products are ready waits for everything — which is exactly how a single missing accessory ends up blocking the delivery of a whole bike.
Delivery dateThe date agreed with the customerIf you set a date earlier than Odoo's own expected date, Odoo warns you that you may not be able to honour it.

5. Sending the quotation and following it up

9
Click Send by e-mail. A standard quotation e-mail template opens — you can personalise the text. The PDF is attached; click it to check how it looks before sending.
10
Watch the chatter: Quotation viewed appears when the customer opens it, and again each time. Frequent re-opening is a buying signal — use it to time your follow-up.
11
If the customer replies through the portal, their message appears in the chatter.
12
Check the envelope icon to confirm the e-mail was actually delivered.
13
Follow up: send a second e-mail after about 3 days, and/or Schedule activity → Call with a date.
Free revenueSystematic follow-up on viewed-but-unanswered quotations is the cheapest sales work available. Nothing in the shop currently does this systematically.

Duplicating a quotation: the cog/gear menu at the top of the record → Duplicate. Useful for variants of the same offer, and for the warranty and leasing routes.
