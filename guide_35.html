Find a bike by serial number

Every bike gets a serial number at manufacturing. It is the most reliable thing in the database — use it before you adjust anything.

1. Where to look

Inventory → Products → Lots / Serial Numbers

From a serial number you can see:

  • the last delivery partner — who has it
  • every transfer it went through: warehouse → partner, returns, internal moves
  • the sales order it belongs to
  • full traceability of movements
Pagination trickThe list is long. Click the record counter (e.g. 1-80 / 273) and type a bigger range to see everything on one page.

2. Uses in daily work

1
A bike is in front of you but your stock says zero → look up the serial number and see where Odoo thinks it is.
2
Before you adjust anything, you need to know what Odoo believes about that bike.
3
Filter the list by location to see, for example, all bikes currently in Louise Stock. Compare that list with the shop floor.
4
A customer arriving for a repair or a warranty claim: the serial number gives you the bike's history, including which order it came from.
RuleNever "correct" a bike's location by guessing. Read the traceability first — it usually tells you exactly what happened.

3. Assigning a serial number before handover

A serial number also has to be assigned to the order before the customer collects, or the POS line stays red and the till step fails. See Confirm the order and prepare the bike.

4. What the numbers do and do not tell you

Treat totals with suspicionLocation figures for bikes were described in training as indicative rather than reliable — for example about 47 in Louise and 80+ in the warehouse, with the honest comment that the figures were probably not exact. The serial-number traceability of an individual bike is far more trustworthy than any location total.

For a live and accurate view of finished bikes, use the internal Production Planner (see The tools around Odoo).
