Book inbound component freight, sea or air. Reads the component needs published by Parts Stock / Parts Agenda (the order tasks) and the sea/air lead times. For each part it works out the sea booking deadline = needed date − sea transit (≈10 wk) − the AND→VV move; if that deadline has already passed, the weeks of production sea can't reach in time are covered by air and the rest by sea. Bookings are grouped by supplier and sent to DSV through your ahooga-dsv-proxy worker — or download the exact booking JSON.
