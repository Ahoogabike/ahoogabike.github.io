How this works: SAV rate = parts replaced after-sales ÷ bikes produced × 100. "Bikes produced" is measured by how many of that part were consumed in production (the parts-stock figure) — so two versions of the same part are counted separately, and for a one-per-bike part like a controller, parts consumed = bikes produced with it. "Parts replaced" counts parts sold via sale orders and parts installed via Repair Orders (the warranty "Add" lines). A rate of 5% means 5 replacements per 100 bikes produced. Accessories (rear rack, bags, locks…) are excluded from this list. Trend shows whether the rate is increasing (⚠ possible quality issue) or decreasing (✓ improving).
