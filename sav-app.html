How this works: SAV rate = parts replaced after-sales ÷ bikes produced that use that part × 100. "Parts replaced" counts both parts sold via sale orders and parts installed via Repair Orders (warranty replacements — the "Add" lines). A rate of 5% means 5 replacement parts per 100 bikes produced. Trend shows whether the rate is increasing (⚠ possible quality issue) or decreasing (✓ improving).
