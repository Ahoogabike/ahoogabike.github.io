Invoice a leasing company and split budgets

The correct invoicing route, and what to do when part of a maintenance job is covered by the lease and part is not.
Never the POSDo not settle a leasing order in the POS. The POS registers the payment as received; the leasing company pays in 30 days. Using the POS creates a false payment in the accounts.

1. The correct invoicing flow

1
On the sales order (customer = leasing company, 30-day terms), click Create Invoice → Regular invoice.
2
Add the labour / service line manually. Services cannot be invoiced automatically from the back office — you cannot ship a service — so the maintenance/labour line will be missing. Add it.
3
Confirm the invoice.
4
Send it by Peppol (and/or e-mail) to the leasing company.
5
Make sure the delivery transfer for the goods is separately validated, so the goods really left your stock.

2. Split budgets: part leasing, part customer

Leasing contracts have maintenance budgets and exclusions. Examples given in training: one partner with a maintenance budget of €150 per year; another that does not include spokes in its maintenance package. So a €160 maintenance job may be €150 to the leasing company and €10 to the rider.

RuleWhen the cost is split, create two sales orders: one to the leasing company for the covered part, one to the rider for the rest. Do not try to split one order between two payers.
6
The leasing-company order → invoice from the back office, 30-day terms, Peppol.
7
The rider's order → settled at the till like any walk-in repair.
Open questionHow to see a rider's remaining maintenance budget is not visible in Odoo — it lives in the leasing company's portal. Confirm per partner how to check a budget before you start work, because the answer determines who gets the bill.

3. Checklist before you close a leasing job

  • The invoice went to the leasing company, not to the rider.
  • The labour line is on the invoice.
  • The delivery transfer is validated in the back office.
  • Nothing was settled in the POS on the leasing company's behalf.
  • If the cost was split, there are two separate orders.
