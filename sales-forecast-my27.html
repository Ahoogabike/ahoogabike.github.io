Season shape Jan → Dec 2027 · financial year
The target is bikes invoiced in calendar 2027, so this is the financial year Jan → Dec. These weights set when that volume lands; they are relative, not absolute — the app normalises them. Dated orders already in Odoo keep their own month and the rest of each SKU's volume is spread by this shape. The PO Builder reads the result and works backwards through production and transit to decide which week each bike has to start — which is how the spring peak gets built the winter before.
Markets
The range does not sell the same way everywhere, so the season is planned country by country. Each market's model split comes from its own 2026 invoiced sales; a market with fewer than 25 bikes last year is too thin a sample to read anything into, so it borrows the overall split and is marked shared. Figures are bikes, with that market's share underneath — so Germany's 2,500 breaks down into the E-MAX and A-MAX you will actually sell there, and then into the four model families. Set the targets here; Other countries is whatever is left of the season total.
Colours
The three stock colours are the only ones we hold inventory in, so they are the only ones with a target you can move. Type units against them and the rest of the plan follows; they are normalised to the stock build, so the season total never stops adding up. Bordeaux Red inherits Ruby Red's 2026 history — it is the colour Ruby Red replaces, and without that it would have no baseline at all. Emerald Green, Atomium and Midnight Blue are shown for completeness but are fixed at what has been pre-ordered, and Custom is set by its own box above.
