Markets
The range does not sell the same way everywhere, so the season is planned country by country. Each market's E-MAX share, Alfine share and rack share come from that market's own 2026 invoiced sales; a market with fewer than 25 bikes last year is too thin a sample to read anything into, so it borrows the overall split and is marked shared.
