How to set MIN / MAX reorder rules in Odoo

Configure automatic replenishment triggers so Odoo suggests purchase orders when stock falls below your defined minimum.

When stock drops to MIN, Odoo automatically suggests a PO to replenish up to MAX.

Applicability: All parts tracked in inventory that are shipped directly from supplier to VV / MTB, or parts typically purchased in quantities above 100 units.

Part 1 — Set the reorder rule on a product

1
Go to Inventory App → Products → open the product you want to configure.
2
Click on the Reordering Rules smart button at the top of the product form (you may need to click the "more" button if it's not visible). If no rules exist yet, the count will show 0.
3
Click New to create a new reorder rule for this product.
4
Fill in the required fields:
FieldWhat to enter
LocationSelect the correct warehouse location (e.g. AND/Stock for Anderlecht, or the subcontracting location)
RouteSelect Buy (this will trigger a PO when stock drops below MIN)
Min QtyThe threshold below which Odoo will suggest a replenishment order. Set based on your safety stock needs.
Max QtyThe target stock level after replenishment. Odoo will suggest ordering up to this level.
Qty MultipleOptional — if the supplier sells in fixed pack sizes (e.g. boxes of 10), enter that number here so orders are always rounded up to a multiple.
5
Make sure the product has a preferred vendor set up. Without a vendor, Odoo cannot generate the purchase order automatically. To check: go to the product form → Purchase tab → confirm a vendor is listed with a price and lead time.
6
Click Save. The reorder rule is now active.
💡 Tip — setting the right MIN: A good starting point is MIN = average weekly consumption × lead time in weeks × 1.2 (safety buffer). For example, if you use 50 units/week and lead time is 6 weeks: MIN = 50 × 6 × 1.2 = 360 units.

Part 2 — Run the replenishment scheduler

7
Go to Inventory App → Operations → Replenishment. This shows all products currently below their MIN quantity.
8
Review the suggested orders. You can adjust the quantity before confirming. Odoo calculates: Order Qty = Max Qty − Current Stock.
9
Click Order Once on individual lines, or Run Scheduler (top right button) to process all suggestions at once. This creates draft Purchase Orders.
10
Go to Purchase App to review the generated draft POs. Confirm the prices with vendors and confirm the orders as per the standard PO process (see Guide 1 or 2).
⚠️ Important: The scheduler creates suggestions, not confirmed orders. Always review before confirming — check vendor prices, lead times, and delivery addresses.

Part 3 — Set lead time on the product

11
On the product form → Purchase tab → vendor line → set the Delivery Lead Time in days. This is the number of days from order confirmation to expected delivery at the warehouse.
12
If the supplier has a history of delays, add 15% buffer to the lead time. For example: if the actual lead time is 90 days and on-time delivery is below 50%, set the lead time to 90 × 1.15 = 104 days.
Where to find this:
Inventory → Products → [select product] → Purchase tab → Vendor Pricelist → Delivery Lead Time

Part 4 — Alternative: set from the Reordering Rules list

13
You can also manage all reorder rules in one place: Inventory → Configuration → Reordering Rules. This gives a full list of all rules across all products, which is useful for bulk review and updates.
14
Use the filters and group-by options to sort by location, product category, or route. You can edit MIN and MAX quantities directly in the list view without opening each product.
💡 Best practice: Review your MIN/MAX settings every time you start a new production order (PO). As production volumes increase, your safety stock levels should increase proportionally.
