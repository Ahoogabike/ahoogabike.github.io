How to make a Purchase Order in odoo that needs to be delivered to VV or MTB?

This process is to make sure all parts are correctly ordered and each step is followed up correctly.

The target is: to ensure that all parts are delivered on time and in the correct quantity and stored correctly in odoo for proper tracking.

Applicability:

⚠️ Key difference from standard PO: Goods are shipped directly from the supplier to VV (Okazi) or MTB (Maastricht) — not to Anderlecht. This applies to parts purchased in quantities above 100 units for subcontracting assembly.
1
All parts that we keep track of the inventory that are shipped from Supplier to VV directly or MTB.
2
All parts that are sellable to dealers or final clients and that we usually purchase it in amounts higher than 100.

The first thing to make sure is that for everybody is clear the full INBOUND Supply process:

Let’s breakdown now each of those steps and clarify what needs to be done:

3
To make a purchase order go in the Purchase Application in odoo.
4
Click on NEW.
5
Write the name of the Vendor from who you are purchasing the items. (Note again: All goods purchased including marketing material / R&D material / everything that we keep track of the stock needs to be registered here).
6
Change the “Deliver to” operation to “Ahooga Anderlecht: Dropship Subcontractor”
7
Add Delivery location “MTB Mastricht” or “Okazi”
8
At that moment check what odoo tells you about the “on time delivery” - if percentage below 50% consider an extra buffer of time of 15% on top of the usual leadtime that each part from that supplier says.
9
Add the date that based on your deadlines to have the order placed (basically what you are targeting to have the order confirmed – it should not be the same day you do it because you are not getting the order confirmed immediately and the leadtime you have set on the product counts starting from the day of your order)
10
Write the product name you want to purchase & the quantity.
11
Add the Vendor Reference, in Vendor Reference you should add as text the “quantity x name of the piece but in a simpler way (ex. Alfine 8 MAX) x POnumber”
12
Add also the project – Project should be the POnumber
13
Now check the “expected time of arrival” - this is based on the leadtime we have at product level. (add 15% delay in time if percentage of on time delivery is lower than 50%)
14
Go in the Tab other information and add payment terms and incoterms.
15
Send by email the RFQ, add to the e-mail also your own personal e-mail xxxx@ahooga.bike in order to have the follow up also via normal e-mail thread.
16
Add in the chat the next activity to do: Confirm price.
17
After price is confirmed, confirm order.
18
When order is confirmed add a new activity: Confirm ETA and put the scheduled deadline to get this information from the Vendor.
19
When you get ETA, click on Receipt and add the scheduled date of the Transfer from “vendor” location to “AND / In Transit”. Go back to the purchase order and schedule a new activity: Confirm Shipment date. Schedule this activity at half way between order and shipment. This will allow you to check with supplier that everything is on time.
20
After confirmation of ETA (if it is kept the same – otherwise change the ETA also in the Receipt delivery) schedule a new Activity on the date of the ETA as “Validate transfer” This will allow you to have an alert when theoretically the shipment should be performed.
21
On the date of the shipment from the Vendor, validate the transfer from “vendor” location to “AND / In Transit”.
22
Set the scheduled date of the transfer from AND/In Transit to the final location.
23
Print the inbound picking operation and put it in the inbound area.
