Take in a repair

How a repair reaches the shop, what to say at drop-off, and how to open the task correctly so the rest of the job runs itself.
Repairs run through the Odoo Project app, in the project Repair Louise. Every repair is a task that moves through kanban stages, and those stages trigger automatic e-mails to the customer.

1. How a repair arrives

A. The customer books online

ahooga.bike → Services → Maintenance → the customer picks a drop-off slot
Why the word "drop-off" mattersThe booking slots are 30 minutes long, so customers used to assume their repair would take 30 minutes and that they could wait for it. The word "drop-off" is there to break that assumption. Reinforce it verbally: "Thank you for dropping off the bike. We need to look at it to see how long it will take, and we will let you know by e-mail when it is ready." A motor problem can take four hours; the customer must not be waiting on a chair for it.
1
The booking lands in the appointment application (the maintenance mailbox). Access is on the main shop computer; ask IT to add it to your own machine.
2
A task is created in Project → Repair Louise, carrying the customer and the appointment date.

B. The repair arrives by phone

3
Customer service answers the phone and books appointments from the office.
Known weak pointThe person on the phone and the person receiving the bike do not always have the same information. When a phone booking arrives, read the notes and re-confirm the job with the customer at drop-off.

2. The daily repair routine

4
In the morning, open Project → Repair Louise and look at today's date: who is coming today, who has already arrived, and who is still expected. Filter or sort the board by date.
5
When the customer arrives, open their task.
6
Note that the task title is currently maintained by hand — appointment date, bike type and customer name are typed in, because the appointment time is not synced onto the task. Keep doing it until it is automated.
7
Take the bike and write in the task what has to be done, as an explicit list. For example: "Basic maintenance; front wheel axle loose; brake pads touching; display buttons stuck; install new lock set; comfort zone; send pro-forma invoice if she wants to pay online."
8
Add any further notes in the description.
9
Tag the bike physically. Under the cashier there are numbered coloured plastic tags. Put one on the bike and write the same identifier in the task — e.g. "24 white". This is how you know which bike is which.
10
Put the bike in the repair area.
11
Optionally assign the task to a mechanic (there is an Assignees field). The shop does not currently do this, but it means each mechanic can see their own list.

3. Check the customer's e-mail address at drop-off

No e-mail address, no communicationIf the contact has no e-mail address, no automatic e-mail can be sent — the customer will never hear that their bike is ready, and will call to complain.

A frequent variant: the e-mail the customer used to book differs from the e-mail on their Odoo contact (they bought the bike years ago with an old address). An automation posts a notification on the task when the booking e-mail and the contact e-mail do not match, so you can confirm with the customer which address to keep. Watch for that notification.

4. What good intake looks like

  • The task exists in Repair Louise with today's date and the customer's name in the title.
  • The work to be done is written as an explicit list, in the task.
  • The bike carries a numbered tag, and the same identifier is in the task.
  • The customer's e-mail address on the contact is correct and confirmed.
  • The customer has been told they will be informed by e-mail, not that they can wait.
