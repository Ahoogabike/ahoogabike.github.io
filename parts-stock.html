Theoretical vs. real stock, per part. Scope: only parts that belong to a MAX bike BoM — every component reached by exploding the bike Bills of Material (all sub-assembly levels), the same parts the Traceability app reaches.
Theoretical = Received − Consumed in production − Sold after-sales is the total quantity that should physically exist. Received is Odoo PO receipts plus any value you type in the ± Adj column (for parts received without a PO or before Odoo started) — adjustments are saved to the shared cloud store (Cloudflare KV) so they persist across devices and teammates. On-hand is split into the AND warehouses (all combined) and LOUI, the locations Odoo can see — neither includes the VV assembly stock. So Theoretical at VV = Theoretical − (AND + LOUI) estimates how many units sit at VV (invisible to Odoo). A negative VV value means Odoo already shows more than the theoretical total — a data gap (scrap, manual adjustments, miscounts), not real VV stock. Manufactured sub-assemblies are tagged and read negative because they are built in-house, not purchased. The last column, Velovisie (Odoo real), is the actual on-hand at the Velovisie subcontracting location as Odoo records it — the real counterpart to compare against the estimated Theoretical at VV.

Forward component stock — week by week. Models the real two-stage flow: incoming component POs (from the PO Tracker) are received into the Anderlecht (AND) warehouse, then moved to Velovisie (VV) for assembly. Each cell is the stock at Velovisie after that week's consumption, replenished from AND via the ⇄ transfer, from the PO Builder — Bikes plan (bikes/week × per-bike BoM usage, averaged per family). (+n) = a component PO arriving into AND that week; ⇄n = the AND→VV transfer needed to keep the assembler supplied — sized to cover ~8 weeks of build (or whatever AND holds), allow ~3 days for the move; ⚙n in week 1 = parts consumed by bikes already in production at Okazi (running POs, not yet built — the future PO-Builder bikes are the weekly plan). A red / negative cell means both AND and VV are exhausted — place or pull a PO forward. POs already received (including those delivered straight to VV via subcontracting) drop out of the open-PO feed and are already reflected in current stock. Per-bike usage is averaged across each family's bikes, so colour/rack-specific parts (paint, racks) are approximate.
Forward wheel-component stock at MTB — week by week. Same two-stage flow as Velovisie, but for the MTB Maastricht wheel-assembly line: wheel components are received into Anderlecht (AND) and moved to MTB, where they're consumed assembling wheels. Wheels are built 3 weeks before the bikes (2 wk build + 1 wk ship), so each cell is the stock at MTB after that week's wheel-assembly consumption, replenished from AND via the ⇄ transfer. (+n) = a component PO arriving into AND; ⇄n = an AND→MTB move. A red / negative cell means both AND and MTB are exhausted — order or pull a component PO forward.
