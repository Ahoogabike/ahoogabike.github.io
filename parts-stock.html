Theoretical vs. real stock, per part. Scope: only parts that belong to a MAX bike BoM — every component reached by exploding the bike Bills of Material (all sub-assembly levels), the same parts the Traceability app reaches.
Theoretical = Received − Consumed in production − Sold after-sales is the total quantity that should physically exist. Received is Odoo PO receipts plus any value you type in the ± Adj column (for parts received without a PO or before Odoo started) — adjustments are saved to the shared cloud store (Cloudflare KV) so they persist across devices and teammates. On-hand is split into the AND warehouses (all combined) and LOUI, the locations Odoo can see — neither includes the VV assembly stock. So Theoretical at VV = Theoretical − (AND + LOUI) estimates how many units sit at VV (invisible to Odoo). A negative VV value means Odoo already shows more than the theoretical total — a data gap (scrap, manual adjustments, miscounts), not real VV stock. Manufactured sub-assemblies are tagged and read negative because they are built in-house, not purchased.

Forward component stock — week by week. Starts from current Velovisie (VV) component stock — the parts physically at the assembler, Theoretical − (AND + LOUI) — and burns each component down using the production plan published by the PO Builder — Bikes app (bikes/week × per-bike BoM usage, averaged per family). (+n) in a cell marks an incoming component PO arriving that week. A red / negative cell means VV runs out that week — pull a delivery forward or place a PO. Per-bike usage is averaged across each family's bikes, so colour/rack-specific parts (paint, racks) are approximate.
