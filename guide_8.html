How to make a Welcome Offer?

1
Go to odoo > Sales App > New quotation
2
Add the new client and click “Create and edit” if the client does not exist yet.
⚠️ Required client fields — must be completed from the start
  • Individual or company (select correctly)
  • Address
  • Zip code
  • City
  • Country
  • VAT Number — mandatory from day one
  • Phone number
  • E-mail
  • Language
If you skip any of these, you will create problems downstream.
Note: if you do not fill in all those info you will create problems afterwards.
3
Click on Sales & Purchase and add the payment items – in red in the pic below.

Set payment terms: 30 Days

Note: you can also setup the fiscal position, this will help ensure the taxes are always correct:

For BE client setup = Regime National

For all other clients = Regime Intracommunataire

Select the correct pricelist you want to assign to this client. There are several pricelist per different countries (ALLY, PRO, ELITE for each country – make sure to select the level of your country)

Click Save & Close.

Now you are back at the sales order.

Since you are doing a welcome offer select the correct welcome offer pricelist depending on your client country. Do it by searching for the word “welcome” in the pricelist tab in the sales order as in the pic below.

Now select the correct quotation template depending for what you want to offer as welcome offer: we have 3 different offers which are including for all of them a Full Set of Accessories + 3 bikes as the combination in the table

Quotation template selection guide:

Quotation Template A-MAX Derailleur A-MAX Alfine E-MAX Derailleur E-MAX Alfine
Welcome offer Mix ✓✓
Welcome offer E-MAX ✓✓✓
Welcome offer A-MAX ✓✓✓

Select the welcome offer you want. Be careful to have done the step before otherwise the prices will br wrong or taxes will be wrong.

If you want to make a different welcome offer that is not existing, I suggest you start from the most similar one and then change just the bikes but leave the discount has it is. You can explain the client that the welcome offer was made with specific constraints but can be modified if needed but then the price will not end with 99.

Click “Send by mail” if you want to send it to the client, Click “Confirm” if you want to confirm it.
