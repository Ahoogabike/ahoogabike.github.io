The tools around Odoo

Odoo is not the only system the shop runs on. These are the others, what each is for, and who gives you access.

1. The internal production & stock tool

An in-house web tool. Log in with your Odoo credentials.

Production Planner → Stock
  • Which finished bikes are in stock and free to use — reserved bikes are already excluded
  • Which bikes are arriving in the next two weeks
Use this, not Odoo, for bikesFor finished bikes this view is more accurate than reading Odoo stock, and it is live. For parts and accessories the tool has no view — use Odoo (see Check stock before you promise anything).

2. The AI assistant with the Ahooga Odoo connector

Ahooga has an AI assistant with a custom connector wired into the Odoo database, with read and write access. Ask the Head of Sales for an account.

What it is good for:

  • Factual questions across the database: "how many helmets have we sold this year?"
  • Serial-number investigations: "I have bike MAX 000712 in front of me. Is it normal that it shows in Louise Stock?" — it will answer, and it can correct the record.
  • Root-cause investigations: for example "why was this bike returned from this dealer?" — the answer being that the dealer had been shipped that bike, wanted one with a rack, so it was swapped and a different bike shipped.
  • Configuration help: routes, operation types, why a transfer behaves as it does.
  • Scheduled reports: a daily list of the bikes you have to deliver today given current stock; mechanics' timesheets; anything recurring.
RuleBefore you spend twenty minutes clicking through transfers to work out what happened to a bike, ask. That is what it is for. Verify anything it proposes to change before you approve it.

3. The SharePoint knowledge library

Reachable from the Ahooga website / internal links. Contains:

  • The warranty process for dealers
  • Terms and conditions
  • Documentation per bike family: Modular, MAX, Folding
  • Ahooga battery care
  • Repair and maintenance documents
This is where a new mechanic learns the bikes. The current team knows them by heart; a new hire will not. Ask the Head of Sales or Marketing / office for access.

4. The appointment booking application

The maintenance mailbox receives website maintenance bookings and feeds the repair tasks. Access via IT — it already exists on the main shop computer; ask for it on your own machine.

5. Typeform

Leasing enquiries from the website (employee and employer forms). Access through the Ahooga Google account — see Work the leasing leads.
