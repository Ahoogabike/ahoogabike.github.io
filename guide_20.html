Confirm the order and prepare the bike

What happens in Odoo when a quotation becomes a sales order, and what has to happen physically before the customer arrives.

1. From quotation to sales order

A quotation becomes a sales order when the customer accepts it in the portal, or when you press Confirm.

1
The customer has an Accept button in the portal. When they use it, the quotation becomes a sales order automatically. Otherwise you confirm it yourself.
2
Confirming creates the delivery (transfer) documents behind the scenes, according to the warehouse and routing settings on the order.
Repairs are sales orders tooWhen you add parts and labour to a repair task, Odoo creates a confirmed sales order automatically. So in the Sales app you will see both real sales and repairs. Filter accordingly when you analyse numbers.

2. Assign the physical bike to the order

Rule — do this before the customer arrivesAssign the physical bike (its serial number) to the order, and put a paper on the bike with the customer name and order number. Then the step at the till takes seconds instead of minutes.
3
Open the delivery/transfer linked to the sales order.
4
Assign the serial number of the bike you are actually going to hand over.
5
Check the line colour. Red means no bike is matched to the order: either the serial number has not been assigned, or you do not have that bike in stock. Fix that before taking the money.
6
Label the bike physically with the customer name and order number.

3. Bike preparation before delivery

  • MAX bikes arrive ready to sell. They are quality-checked before shipment; in principle you unbox, remove the plastic wrap, and the bike is ready.
  • Anything sold as a separate line is not assembled: kickstand, racks (unless the "with rack" variant was ordered), ponchos, bags, accessories. You assemble what the customer bought.
  • Accessories that are part of the order should be fitted or bagged and kept with the bike, so the handover is one movement.
Open pointDealers report taking the MAX out of the box and selling it directly; the shop currently spends about 25–30 minutes per bike before delivery. What is actually necessary should be verified and agreed, because it is up to 30 minutes of labour per bike.

4. Before you call the customer to collect

  • The bike is physically present, prepared and labelled with the customer name and order number.
  • The serial number is assigned on the delivery, and the line is not red.
  • Every accessory on the order is either present or removed from the order with a decision written in a log note.
  • Warehouse on the order is Louise and Sales Team is Point of Sale Louise.
  • Any down payment is recorded (see Take a down payment).
