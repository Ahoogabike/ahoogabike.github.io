Ahooga Spares & Accessories
Odoo: not connected Repair demand: —
Replenish accessories, and forecast spare-part repair demand. Demand is forecast from each item's sales velocity (recent sale orders, window set in Settings) — type a manual override to pin a value. For accessories it uses a min/max rule: Min = velocity × (lead + safety) is the reorder point; when stock + on order drops below Min it orders up to Max = velocity × (lead + safety + cover). Between Min and Max nothing is ordered. Proposals are grouped by vendor. Spare parts are the MAX BoM components, so they're ordered once through Parts Stock — here we only forecast their repair demand and publish it to Parts Stock so it adds it on top of production (no double-ordering).
