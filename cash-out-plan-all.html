The bottom table is IN − OUT building on your opening bank balance (auto from Odoo/KBC). Accounts receivable is shown but excluded from the opening by default — it's permanent working capital that stays roughly constant.
issued POs = open purchase orders in Odoo, paid at issue date + that vendor's usual payment terms. planned POs = the Parts Agenda's upcoming orders — each part's needed-date − lead time gives the issue date, then + the vendor's usual terms — valued at part cost × qty. payroll (class 62) and services/other (class 61/64/65) run at their monthly run-rate from accounting. POs are paid in milestones — e.g. 30% deposit at order, 70% balance at shipment (expected receipt − transit) — not all in one month. Turn the split off in Settings to use single net-terms payments instead. All ex-VAT unless toggled.
