Glossary

TermMeaning
ActivityA dated to-do on a record (call, e-mail, meeting). Turns red when overdue.
AnderlechtAhooga's HQ and warehouse. Referred to in Odoo as the warehouse.
BOM (Bill of Materials)The list of components that make up an assembled product.
BreadcrumbThe clickable navigation path at the top-left of an Odoo screen.
Delivery / Transfer / PickingThe stock document that moves goods between locations. Must be validated for stock to change.
Down paymentPartial advance payment on an order. Minimum guideline around 20%.
Fiscal positionThe tax regime applied to a customer; determines VAT.
FollowersPeople notified of changes to a record.
KanbanCard-and-column view; used for repair stages.
Lot / Serial numberUnique identifier of an individual item; every bike has one from manufacturing.
Log noteInternal comment on a record. Not sent to the customer.
LouiseThe current flagship store. Louise Stock is the shop's stock location.
Marolles / MarolThe new store location.
Operation typeThe kind of stock document: receipt, delivery, internal transfer, request, POS picking.
PeppolEuropean e-invoicing network; used for B2B invoices.
PivotCross-tab reporting view.
POS / Point of SaleThe till application.
Price listA set of prices/margins per customer type. B2C uses the Public Pricelist.
Repair LouiseThe Odoo project containing all shop repairs.
Reordering ruleAutomatic restocking when a product falls below a minimum.
ReservedStock physically present but already promised to an order.
Route / RoutingThe rule set that decides how goods move (1-step, 2-step…). Set per product category.
Sales teamRevenue attribution group. The shop is Point of Sale Louise.
Session (POS)One opening-to-closing cycle of the till.
Smart buttonThe counters at the top of a record that jump to related records.
VariantA specific version of a product (colour, size, with/without rack). Stock lives on the variant.
