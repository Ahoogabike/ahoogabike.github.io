Handle helpdesk tickets and customer questions

You do not open tickets — you get pulled into them. Here is how they work and what is expected of you.

1. How tickets reach you

Helpdesk → My Tickets
  • You are not responsible for opening tickets. Customer service does that.
  • You get involved when someone assigns a ticket to you or tags you — typically for questions like "when will my bike be delivered / be ready?", "can I book an appointment?", "can you repair my bike in the shop?".
  • You are notified inside Odoo. Use My Tickets to see yours; you can also look at a colleague's tickets to balance the load.

2. Working a ticket

A ticket works exactly like a project task: messages, log notes, activities.

1
Read the whole thread before replying — the customer has often already been answered once.
2
Open the customer's contact and read the smart buttons: sales, POS tickets, invoiced total, repair tasks. Most delivery and repair questions are answered there.
3
Answer the customer with Send message. Use Log note for anything internal.
4
If you cannot answer today, Schedule activity with a date rather than leaving it open.
WarningSend message e-mails the customer; Log note does not. Check which tab is active before you type.

3. Questions you can answer yourself

"When will my bike be ready?"Open Project → Repair Louise, find the task, read the stage. Do not promise a time if the card is in Missing component.
"When will my order be delivered?"Open the sales order → the delivery → check whether lines are red, and check the realistic warehouse lead time (see Check stock before you promise anything).
"I never received your e-mail"Check the envelope icon in the chatter and the e-mail address on the contact. A red envelope means it never arrived — fix the contact and resend.
"What did you do to my bike?"Read the task description. This is why it must be written up.
"Can I book an appointment?"Point the customer to the online booking page, or book it in the appointment application.

4. Keep the follower list sane

Why notifications get ignoredAnyone tagged on a record becomes a follower and receives an e-mail for every later change. Long follower lists generate notifications that are not for the reader, and those stop being read. Tag deliberately, and remove followers who do not need to be there.
