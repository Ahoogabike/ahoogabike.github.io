Ahooga PO Builder — Long-lead
BETA · compare vs old builder Not connected Plans: —
Bulk component ordering for long-lead suppliers. Sizes one consolidated draft PO per supplier to cover a rolling window of production, instead of small per-part top-ups. Quantity = (bikes in the coverage window × per-bike BoM usage) + after-sales spares − (on-hand + open POs), rounded up to a clean lot. Bike volume combines the current-year plan (production_plan) and the MY27 plan (production_plan_my27) so growth is included. Spares scale with that future volume from each part's historical after-sales rate. Colour variants (e.g. the frame) are combined into one total — allocate colours in Odoo before confirming. This is a BETA tool: it creates draft POs only.
