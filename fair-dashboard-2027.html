Budget estimation

Live estimate for the fairs you've ticked, built from real drivers: Stand space = m² × €/m², Stand build = m² × €/m², Accommodation = people × nights × hotel rate, Meals = people × days × per-diem, plus transport (by van × country distance) and marketing. People come from who's assigned on the Staffing grid (or HR-needed); nights from each fair's dates; m² is set per fair (defaults by van size until you enter it). Edit the global rates in Assumptions, the m² per fair in the table, or type over any single figure to override it. Dealer fees are income and subtracted for the net.

Staffing grid — who goes where

Fairs are rows (with their dates), people are columns. Click a cell to assign / unassign someone — it saves to that fair's employee presence and syncs to the cloud. Cells turn red ⚠ when that person is also on another fair within a week, and amber within two weeks, so you can spot overloaded people. Only ticked fairs appear here.

Van routes & mileage

Three vans: a big van for Germany & Austria, a small van for the UK, and a small van for Belgium & Netherlands. Each visits its ticked fairs in date order. Between fairs the German van returns to Stuttgart / München only when there's a long gap (> 10 days); the UK van waits in London and the BE/NL van in Brussels between fairs. Lines are trips (labelled with the destination fair's date); mileage is straight-line × 1.3 as a rough road estimate. Untick a fair on the tracker and it drops out of the route. Sweden is not covered by these vans.
