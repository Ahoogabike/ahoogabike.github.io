3-way match — pay only what's right. Each vendor bill line is matched to its purchase-order line and the goods received. It flags overcharges (invoiced unit price > the agreed PO price) and over-billing (invoiced qty > received). Currencies are handled: bills in USD, CNY, TWD… are converted to ${"€"} (using the rate Odoo booked on the bill, or the live rate for drafts) and compared against the EUR PO price, so a foreign bill is no longer falsely flagged. The original currency and the €-equivalent are both shown. Tolerance avoids rounding/FX noise.
Negotiate where it counts. Per part: current price, the price trend over time, your annual spend (qty × price — where the money is), and your volume growth (your leverage). Sorted by spend. Flags: 💰 high spend 📈 volume up ⬆ price up — these are the parts to push on.
