2027 growth by country drives the forecast
These are your bike growth assumptions per country for next year. In the attach-rate model, accessories scale with bikes, so a country selling +100% more bikes is forecast to sell +100% more accessories (attach rate held constant). Defaults: Germany +100%, United Kingdom +200%, Belgium +30%, all others 0%. Edit any value and the whole forecast updates. New dealers/shops are captured automatically through higher bike volume.
