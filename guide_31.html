Write a leasing / B2B order

A leasing order carries two customers in one document: the payer and the rider. Getting that structure right is the whole job.
Why this matters more than its length suggestsBike leasing is roughly half of the sales in a comparable Belgian bike retail business. In the shop in 2026, leasing sales amounted to about one bike. Treat leasing as a growth project, not an administrative annoyance.

1. The leasing partners

Ahooga works with several leasing companies — among them O2O, KBC Autolease and Cyclis, plus others. Check the current list with the Head of Sales before quoting, and note that the shop can open a contract with as many leasing companies as it wants; that decision sits with the shop.

2. How to write the order

1
Create the quotation with the leasing company as the Customer (e.g. KBC Autolease).
2
Set the Delivery address to the rider — the employee who gets the bike. This is who the bike physically goes to. Make sure the rider's contact record is complete.
3
Set the Invoice address to the leasing company.
4
Put the rider's name in Customer reference (Other Info tab) so you can find the order by person.
5
Payment terms: leasing companies are typically 30 days.
6
Send the quotation — the e-mail goes to the leasing company's contact.
So a leasing order carries two customers in one document: the payer and the rider.

3. On the portal side

Ahooga uploads the signed contract to the leasing company's portal, not the quotation.

Improvement to exploreThe uncertainty in this flow is knowing whether the employer / leasing company will approve the request before you have committed. In other retail networks the quotation is uploaded so approval comes first. This is worth taking up directly with the leasing partners.

4. Do not settle a leasing order at the till

WarningDo not settle a leasing order in the POS. The POS registers the payment as received, and the leasing company has not paid — they pay in 30 days. Using the POS here creates a false payment in the accounts. Invoice it from the back office instead: see Invoice a leasing company.

5. Delivering the bike to the rider

  • The delivery address on the order is the rider, not the leasing company.
  • The rider's contact has a correct e-mail address and language, so they receive their documents.
  • The serial number is assigned on the delivery before handover.
  • The delivery transfer is validated in the back office — not through the POS.
  • Sales Team is Point of Sale Louise so the shop is credited with the sale.
