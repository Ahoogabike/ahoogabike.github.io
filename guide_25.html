POS vs back office: keep stock right and investigate a red picking

The single mechanism you must understand to keep shop stock correct — and how to clean up when it goes wrong.

1. What happens to stock when you validate a POS order

1
When you confirm a sales order in the back office, Odoo creates a delivery transfer (e.g. Louise Stock → Customer), waiting to be validated.
2
If the customer collects in the shop and you settle the order in the POS, the POS creates its own picking (Louise/POS) and the back-office delivery is automatically cancelled. The two documents contain the same goods; only the reference differs.
3
The POS picking is what depletes your shop stock.
The golden rule of the shopCustomer collects in the shop → deliver through the POS.
Goods are shipped → validate the delivery in the back office.
Never both.
What goes wrongIf you validate the back-office delivery and settle the order in the POS, the POS still creates its picking, but it stays unvalidated (shown red) because the goods have already left stock. The fix is to cancel that leftover POS picking. The cause is almost always an unavailable line on the order — see Settle an order and hand over at the till, section 5.

2. Investigate a red picking

A red picking inside a POS session means: the customer paid and took the goods, but the stock transfer out of Louise Stock was never validated. The money is in, the goods are gone, and Odoo still believes the items are on your shelf.

4
Open the session → find the red picking → note the products and serial numbers.
5
Check the corresponding order in Orders: is it invoiced and paid? (Usually yes.)
6
Establish why the transfer failed. Typical causes:
  • One line was not in stock in the system, and the shipping policy was When all products are ready, so one missing item blocked the whole delivery.
  • The bike was physically taken from the warehouse and given to the customer without ever being registered into Louise Stock, so the system did not know it was there.
  • Someone validated the delivery in the back office instead, leaving the POS picking orphaned — in that case simply cancel the picking.
7
Correct it, and log a note explaining what you did.

3. Where to find them

Point of Sale → Sessions → open a session → look at the pickings
RoutineReview red pickings across the week's POS sessions every week. It is a short list if you do it weekly and an archaeology project if you do not.
