Handle a warranty job

The customer pays nothing — but the goods still have to move in Odoo. This is where the shop loses the most stock accuracy.
Warranty cover, for reference5 years frame and fork; 2 years on other components including battery and motor. Wear parts, misuse, improper assembly, accident and modification are excluded. The detailed warranty process and the dealer warranty documentation live in the SharePoint knowledge library.
Still to be trainedHow to assess whether a case is warranty or not, and what information to collect, is covered in a separate session. Ask the Head of Sales for it, and for the written description of the agreed warranty treatment.

1. Book the repair as normal

1
Exactly like any repair (see Take in a repair): the customer books, the task is created, you record the job — e.g. "MAX controller broken".
2
Add the parts and the labour. Odoo creates the sales order — for example €169,85 of parts + €59,95 of labour.
3
Move the task to Warranty assessment while it is being judged.

2. Zero the order — always in two lines

The customer must pay nothing, so you add negative lines:

ProductQuantityPrice
Warranty for labour1− the labour amount (e.g. −59,95)
Warranty for parts1− the parts amount (e.g. −169,85)
Rule — never net it out in one lineLabour and parts are different in the accounts: a service versus goods, different accounts, different cost analysis. Always use the two separate warranty products so warranty cost can be split into labour and material.

3. The €0 problem, and the two ways round it

The problem: an order whose total is €0 cannot be found or settled in the POS. The POS registers payments; there is nothing to pay. So the "normal" route is closed.

Route B — through the POS (preferred)

The idea: keep the order non-zero when you settle it, then apply the warranty credits at the till so the total lands on 0.

4
On the sales order, keep the parts and labour lines (the order has a positive value). Add a note/reference such as "warranty" and Confirm.
5
Point of Sale → Actions → Quotation/Order → enter the order number → Settle the order. Because the order is not €0, it is found normally.
6
In the POS, add the Warranty for labour and Warranty for parts products as negative lines (quantity 1, price −59,95 and −169,85). The ticket total becomes 0.
7
Payment → Bank → Validate.

The result is a €0 ticket that nevertheless moves the goods out of stock and records the labour — which is exactly what was missing before.

Set-up noteFor this to work, the two warranty products must be available in the Point of Sale (the Point of Sale checkbox on the product form). If a warranty product does not appear at the till, that is the cause.

Route A — back office (necessary when the goods are shipped)

8
Handle the delivery entirely in the back office and make sure that every transfer line on that sales order is set to Done.
WarningIf a transfer is not Done, no stock depletion happens — the part is fitted to the customer's bike and still counted in your stock. This is the single biggest source of wrong stock in the shop: at one point there were more than 170 open transfers from Louise with the goods already delivered. Note also that some products are configured as two-step deliveries, so a single warranty order can generate more than one transfer — all of them must be Done.
Which routeRoute B is the agreed way forward for warranty on a walk-in customer. Route A remains necessary whenever the goods are shipped rather than collected.

4. The mistake to watch for

The classic warranty error"It's a warranty, the bike is free, hand it over and start the next one." The parts are never delivered in the system. Your stock is wrong from that moment on, and it stays wrong until someone spends a day investigating.
Make it a habitA warranty job is not finished until the goods have moved in Odoo.
