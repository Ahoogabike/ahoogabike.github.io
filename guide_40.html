Troubleshooting: symptoms, causes, fixes

Find your symptom in the left column. Most shop problems in Odoo are one of these.

Odoo and access

SymptomLikely causeFix
An application is missing from my Odoo home screenAccess rights, or you are in the other environmentCheck the URL first, then ask the Head of Sales — rights are granted per environment. See Odoo essentials.
The customer received an internal remarkYou used Send message instead of Log noteNothing to undo — apologise if needed. Check the active tab before typing next time.
Customer says "I never got the e-mail"Wrong e-mail on the contact, or the booking e-mail differs from the contact e-mailCheck the red envelope icon in the chatter; look for the mismatch notification on the task; fix the contact and resend.
An automatic e-mail went out in the wrong languageLanguage not set on the contactSet the Language field on the contact. See Create and maintain a customer contact.
VAT on an order is wrongCountry or fiscal position missing on the contactSet the country, then the fiscal position.

Orders, till and invoices

SymptomLikely causeFix
I cannot find an order in the POSIts total is €0 — typically a warrantyUse the warranty route: keep the order positive, settle it, then add the negative warranty lines at the till. See Handle a warranty job.
No invoice was created for a POS saleThe Invoice button was not clicked at paymentAlways click Invoice before choosing the payment method. See Settle an order and hand over at the till.
An invoice is missing the labourServices are not invoiced automatically from the back officeAdd the labour line manually before confirming. See Invoice from the back office.
Revenue is not showing in the shop's numbersSales Team on the order is not Point of Sale LouiseFix the field, and audit older orders. See Sales reporting and saved filters.
A duplicate product appeared in the catalogueSomeone pressed Enter on free text in an order lineArchive it and tell the Head of Sales. Always select an existing product.
A duplicate contact exists for one customerCreated without checking the proposals firstDo not merge blindly — flag it, because a customer's history is split across both records.

Stock and transfers

SymptomLikely causeFix
A product line is red in a quotation or the POSNot in stock, already reserved, or no serial number assignedCheck Inventory → Products → On Hand; assign the serial number on the delivery. See Confirm the order and prepare the bike.
A picking is red in a POS sessionPaid and handed over, but the stock transfer was never validatedInvestigate per POS vs back office — usually a blocked line or double handling.
Stock says we have a part, the shelf is emptyFitted to a repaired bike awaiting collection, or given out without a transferSee Add parts and labour and Do a physical inventory count.
Stock shows a negative quantitySomething was sold that the system did not haveCorrect in the physical inventory and find the missing receipt.
A bike is physically here but Odoo says it is elsewhereAn untracked moveLook up the serial number's traceability before adjusting anything. See Find a bike by serial number.
Old Waiting transfers from Louise StockOrders never closed, accessories never collected, duplicatesWork the list monthly. See Stock discipline and stale transfers.
I need a bike today and it is not in the shopNot possible — 3–4 days from the warehouse (up to a week today)Tell the customer the real date; keep the right stock in the shop. See Check stock before you promise anything.
